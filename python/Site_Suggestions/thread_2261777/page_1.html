Koenigtiamat  pazil 

 For me "bad tabs" can be done away with. 

Why? 1) If a beginning tab author submits a tab it is approved ie checked, corrected and after that approved.  OR if you have a more experienced tab author that can directly submit tabs these authors have achieved a certain level of experience and knowledge to produce good level tabs .

So why are bad tabs needed at all?

My experience is that bad tabs are given by raters who do not have any experience either in making tabs or/and even in playing guitar.  

Why is it we authors are "checked" for the tabs we submit while the raters can just do anything without being checked?

Examples: 
1)  Should a starter  guitar player be allowed to rate a  tab? For me no.
2)  Should a rater be allowed to give a bad tab because there is one spelling mistake? No (but it does happen unfortunately)
3)  Should a rater be allowed to give a bad tab report because they "think it doesn't sound right"? (happens often) Again for me No.
4)  Should a rater be allowed to give a bad tab because the rater lacks the skills to play the song? Needless to say for me also a No.
5)  Why would level 5 authors with over say over 1000 tabs on the site and with a very good rating record be allowed to receive bad tabs or bad ratings  at all?

Unfortunately exactly the same happens with the rating system.  A lot of mis-use and  misunderstanding.

So for me there is something not correct as authors (simply put) are "checked" before a tab is put on the site while for raters it is a free for all ie there is no check whatsoever on their skills. 

I refer to earlier discussions & contrubutions (starting #2653) on this concern in this thread : https://www.ultimate-guitar.com/forum/showthread.php?t=1832452&page=67#2655

EDIT: So to be clear my site suggestion is: 
1)   to do away with the bad tab option either the button or the one linked to the 1 star rating, and
2)   no ratings lower than 3 without a (good & hopefully valid) comment as to why this low rating was given. (this comment should thus increase the quality of the tab).

My view is every user can have an opinion on what to rate a tab ,however on occasion the overall rating of a tab can be warped,by reluctance to never accept anything other than a 5 star,or newbies that do not understand the rating system,revenge/spite rates,the latter ones generally can easily be removed with the help of emad 

As an example you have users that only ever accept 5 star ratings for their tabs,and will always ask for 3 star ratings to be removed as well as 2 and 1 star rates ,this often skews an overall rating ,and in essence some of those tabs could and should possibly be 3 or 4 star rates.

On the other side,some  3 star ratings can be given from newbies that dont really understand how the system works,the wording i think for 3 star rating is "worth Learning" which then users think is an OK rating,which then really is not a good rating for what might well be a good tab.Maybe this could be reworded to "Average"I do think moderators and above should be able to delete wrong or revenge bad tab comments ,as these can often be unfair.Unfortunately i think the size of the site and amount of users it has ,these problems are very hard to iron out.I do think all raters that are registered should have the ability to rate,but where anyone puts a bad tab report,off the back of 1 or 2 star ratings,once viewed and moderated the report should I feel be able to able be deleted,often these can be revenge,spam,or learners frustrated at not being able to play the tab,It is not often that the comments added to the bad tab report are constructive.
