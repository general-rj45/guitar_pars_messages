Yo.

I have some criticism to offer for the UG Guitar Team if that's okay.

I understand the workload the UG team is under (the amount (and variety! ) of tabs you people crank out every week is pretty astounding and I really do commend that!), but I have to point out that the quality of the official tabs is a bit questionable at times, especially considering that they are automatically tagged "high quality" and are advertised as such.

What I mean is that I was under the impression that the official tabs are supposed to offer comprehensive tabs that are clearly distinguishable from the basic stuff submitted by users here. Of course no tab is perfect and everyone makes mistakes (and some songs do have way too many tracks to fit in a tab, so completely comprehensive tabs are not always possible), but if I'm being 100 % honest, some of the official tabs just seem plain lazy to me.

Here's two examples to illustrate what I'm getting at:

1. The recent official tab for Simple and Clean by Utada Hikaru.

The original song is full of all kinds of small flourishes often present in modern pop production and most of those are ignored entirely in the official tab (even the backing vocals are almost completely missing!?). There are very basic mistakes with the rhythm of the vocal melody etc. There are only 3 tracks in the official tab that are not vocals or percussion (8 tracks in the tab overall, whereas just the synth tracks of the actual song should make up at least 7 tracks by themselves. And that's just with a quick listen). I'm sorry but I don't see how that counts as "high quality".

2. The official tab for Harvest by Opeth.

The time signature is very obviously wrong in multiple sections (this is evident if one tries to relate the tab player's metronome to the song's main pulse) and the hi-hats on the drum track are not at all what they are supposed to be. These are mistakes that wouldn't be that unusual in user-submitted tabs, but in official tabs? C'mon. I remember reading that some of you have degrees in music so it shouldn't be a matter of skill.

Of course there's the fact that I view tabs (and especially official tabs) as more than just specifically GUITAR tabs and would like the official tabs to be fairly consistent with all elements of the song, minor flourishes included. They are often useful with picking up on other stuff going on in the song, if, for example, trying to learn the song with a full band setup. Obviously if one is of the opinion that the accuracy of the guitar part is the only thing that matters, then I suppose it's just a difference of opinion. But as long as the intention is to create as full of a representation of the song as reasonably possible, it takes more than that.

I know there are a shit ton of requests to cover and that making properly comprehensive tabs takes a lot of time. But still, I personally would be completely okay with some tabs taking significantly longer to create than they currently do if that meant that they would be comprehensive and more consistently accurate. The tab will be useful forever once it's created, after all!

Thanks for reading    I love the site and I really appreciate the work you all do – this piece is by no means intended to provoke or anything. Just that at least for me, the quality should be top priority if I've understood the concept of official tabs properly.

P.S. There are countless great official tabs and this criticism does not apply to most, obviously. Some of them just have really weird omissions or blatant errors that seem very out of place in official tabs.
